Who We Help

Support for families navigating difficult seasons.

The and More Project supports individuals and families who may be experiencing crisis, hardship, displacement, or a major life transition.

Single mothers who have limited family, financial, or community support.

Women and families impacted by domestic violence.

Children experiencing displacement or significant family transitions.

Families working toward greater stability and independence after hardship.

Need some support?

Assistance is based on individual circumstances and available resources. Reaching out does not guarantee financial or material assistance, but we will do our best to listen, respond compassionately, and connect families with available support.

Why We Began

Everyone deserves to know they are more.

The and More Project was created from a simple belief: difficult circumstances should never define a person's worth or determine their future.

Too many women, mothers, children, and families face major life transitions without the support system they need. Sometimes a fresh start begins with something practical — toiletries, clothing, a warm blanket, school supplies, a tank of gas, or simply knowing that someone cares.

Our goal is to meet people where they are, provide practical help when we can, and remind each person we serve that their current circumstances are only part of their story.

Your Safety Matters

The and More Project is not an emergency or crisis-response service. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a life-threatening emergency, call 911.

For domestic violence support, contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-SAFE (7233) or text START to 88788.
