"The and More Project empowers single mothers with limited support and families impacted by domestic violence by providing practical assistance, essential resources, comfort, and compassionate support as they rebuild their lives and move toward stability and independence—because they are more than the circumstances they have faced."

How We Serve

Our holistic approach addresses immediate needs while helping build a foundation for lasting stability.

Comfort & Essentials

Providing hygiene packs, warm blankets, clothing, baby supplies, and comfort packages to restore immediate dignity during displacement or acute hardship.

Educational Resources

Equipping mothers and children with school supplies, learning materials, and developmental guidance to build resilience and foster long-term growth.

Transition Assistance

Delivering limited emergency assistance and relocation starter kits to help bridge the critical gap between crisis and permanent stability.
